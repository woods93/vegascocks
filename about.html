About

The Vegas Cocks are a street-smart, mid-90s rock & roll band out of Nashville, Tennessee, with the emphasis on rock.

Where the songs came from

  • Too Late was a vinyl 45 mixed by Paul Mahern and released by Ramona Records of Indianapolis.
  • Three O’Clock Love came out of a development deal with MCA Records and was produced by Brad Circone of the Toll.
  • Rainbow Toupe was produced by Joe Baldridge.
